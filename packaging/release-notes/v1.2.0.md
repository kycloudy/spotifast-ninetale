Spotifast Ninetale 1.2.0 brings in Spotifast 0.15 and 0.16. It adds Spotify Soloist playback, Windows Media Player skins, more on artist pages, and storage sizes in Settings, with the Ninetale look and in-app updates unchanged.

Spotifast Ninetale is for Windows. Download the installer (`.msi` or `-setup.exe`) or the portable zip for your processor: `x86_64` for most PCs, `aarch64` for Windows on ARM.

**Download:** [all files for this release](https://github.com/kycloudy/spotifast-ninetale/releases/tag/v1.2.0)

## New

- **Listen through Spotify Soloist,** Spotify's own player, with a guided setup. The built-in player stays available at every step. Spectrum, oscilloscope and MilkDrop follow Soloist's audio on Windows 11; audible EQ needs the built-in player. Soloist needs Spotify Premium and your own Soloist key, and Spotify supplies it for x64 Windows only. ([setup and limits](https://spotifast.rocks/soloist/))
- **Windows Media Player skins** for the mini player, as well as Winamp skins.
- **Artist pages show monthly listeners and upcoming releases,** with a countdown to the release day.
- **See your storage sizes and clear cached audio** in Settings.
- **Copy a song's details** from a submenu in the song menu.
- **Sort an album's songs by plays.**
- **Links in a playlist's description open** when you click them.
- **Other devices' changes appear at once,** as Spotify sends them.

## Fixed

- **Lyrics recover from LRCLIB overloads,** and cached lyrics stay available during outages.
- **Liked Songs plays the whole collection** and marks the playing row correctly.
- **Local song names show spaces and accents correctly.**
- **A remote volume holds** through a device that reports it late.
- **Hidden songs are marked** in rows that load after the first page.
- **The artist page keeps its scroll position** when a discography category loads.
- **Update checks and downloads share one connection.**
- Many more fixes from [Spotifast 0.15.0](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.15.0) and [0.16.0](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.16.0).
