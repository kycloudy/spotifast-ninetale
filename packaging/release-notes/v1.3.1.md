Spotifast Ninetale 1.3.1 brings in Spotifast 0.17.2. It gets Soloist playing again on Windows, fixes songs that would not start, and adds a checklist of your playlists to the heart. The Ninetale look and in-app updates are unchanged.

Spotifast Ninetale is for Windows. Download the installer (`.msi` or `-setup.exe`) or the portable zip for your processor: `x86_64` for most PCs, `aarch64` for Windows on ARM.

**Download:** [all files for this release](https://github.com/kycloudy/spotifast-ninetale/releases/tag/v1.3.1)

## New

- **The heart opens a checklist of your playlists,** with their covers. Tick or untick Liked Songs and any playlist in one place.
- **Updates can install themselves.** Spotifast downloads a new version and installs it the next time it starts, or right away if you restart. Settings has an **Update automatically** switch.

## Fixed

- **Soloist plays again on Windows.** Spotify's newest Soloist build crashes as it starts. A new build that keeps crashing now sends Spotifast back to the last build that worked.
- **A song picked on your phone plays,** not the first song of the playlist.
- **Songs start again after autoplay took over.** With shuffle on, Soloist and other devices no longer refuse every new song.
- **Clicking a song far down the queue plays only that song.**
- **Soloist ends with Spotifast,** however Spotifast ends. A forced quit or a crash no longer leaves the player running.
- **No more rectangular shadow round skins.**
- **A Sonos started from your phone can be controlled,** and its volume holds.
- **Next up no longer lists a song ten times,** and **Recent shows each play once.**
- **The sign-in screen shows the whole redirect address again.**
