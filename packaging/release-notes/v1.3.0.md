Spotifast Ninetale 1.3.0 brings in Spotifast 0.17. It adds skins that run as Windows Media Player made them, two new skins with MilkDrop inside, a new Soloist setup, and a full screen lyrics button, with the Ninetale look and in-app updates unchanged.

Spotifast Ninetale is for Windows. Download the installer (`.msi` or `-setup.exe`) or the portable zip for your processor: `x86_64` for most PCs, `aarch64` for Windows on ARM.

**Download:** [all files for this release](https://github.com/kycloudy/spotifast-ninetale/releases/tag/v1.3.0)

## New

- **Windows Media Player skins run as they were made.** Drawers, hidden panels, separate windows and shaped windows work. Scripts run in a sandbox with no access to your files, the network or other programs.
- **Two new skins, Spotifast Tall and Spotifast Wide,** with MilkDrop in their glass. New installs open the mini player in Spotifast Wide. A skin you already chose stays.
- **See your skins before you wear them.** Settings shows a picture of each skin.
- **MilkDrop in skins.** It fills a skin's visualization area, and sits in the Winamp skin's frame under the equalizer and playlist.
- **MilkDrop behind your lyrics.** Press **V** in full screen lyrics, or click an empty space. **N** and **P** change the preset. Click the album art to see MilkDrop alone.
- **A full screen button in the player bar** opens and leaves full screen lyrics.
- **A new Soloist setup** with three steps, and clearer Soloist settings. If Soloist stops, Spotifast stays open and restarts it.
- **Open a Spotify link** from the search bar. Paste it and press Enter.
- **Shift+Q adds the selected songs to the queue.** Shift+N plays them next.
- **Compilations** show on artist pages.

## Fixed

- **Soloist keeps playing smoothly** on a busy computer, and returns at the volume you left it.
- **Spotify lyrics load again** for songs that showed none.
- **A busy lyrics service no longer shows an error.**
- **MilkDrop starts after an update** replaces the running app.
- **The playing playlist is remembered** across a restart, and a song shows as playing only in the playlist it plays from.
- Many more fixes from [Spotifast 0.17.0](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.17.0) and [0.17.1](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.17.1).
